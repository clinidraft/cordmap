# CordMap

An interactive guide to spinal cord injury levels.

Pick a level on the spine (C1 to S4–5) and the page shows which parts of the body are affected, what still works (breathing, hands, trunk, legs, bladder and bowel, blood pressure) and the typical way of getting around after a complete injury at that level.

It also explains the ASIA Impairment Scale (A to E), with a step-by-step helper, and the main incomplete injury patterns (central cord, Brown-Séquard, anterior cord, posterior cord) on a cross-section of the cord.

Built by Suriya as part of a portfolio for a medicine application.

## Sources

- International Standards for Neurological Classification of Spinal Cord Injury (ISNCSCI), American Spinal Injury Association: key muscles, key sensory points, AIS grades.
- Consortium for Spinal Cord Medicine, guidance on outcomes after complete traumatic spinal cord injury (Paralyzed Veterans of America): typical function by level.

## Limits

For learning only. Not a diagnostic tool. The body map is simplified; real dermatomes overlap and vary between people. Incomplete injuries vary widely.
